Description: 

• Designed and published a comprehensive Tableau dashboard tracking confirmed cases, recoveries, fatalities, and
vaccination rates across all Indian states.
• Implemented choropleth heat maps, trend lines, and dual-axis charts to visualise state-wise infection growth patterns,
mortality rates, and recovery trajectories over time.
• Derived actionable insights on pandemic progression milestones and vaccination coverage gaps, producing a public-
awareness report to support data-driven health policy discussions.
